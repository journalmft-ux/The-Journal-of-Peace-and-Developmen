# Welcome to The Journal of Peace and Development
### Fostering Stability through Governance and Progress

The **Journal of Peace and Development (JPD)** is an online publication platform that provides scholars and researchers an accessible platform for the dissemination of academic work. Our focus is on Open Access, Peer Review, and promoting sustainable development in the Horn of Africa.
