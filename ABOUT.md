  <h1 style="color: #064e3b; border-bottom: 2px solid #064e3b;">About the Journal</h1>
  
  <p>The <strong>Journal of Peace and Development (JPD)</strong> is a multidisciplinary, peer-reviewed journal established in 2026. It serves as a platform for scholars, researchers, and practitioners to share insights on governance, peacebuilding, and sustainable development, with a primary focus on the Horn of Africa.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #064e3b;">Our Mission</h3>
  <p>To promote evidence-based research that contributes to stability, effective public administration, and socio-economic progress in developing nations.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #064e3b;">Focus and Scope</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Public Administration and Governance</li>
    <li>Conflict Resolution and Peace Studies</li>
    <li>Traditional Leadership and Modern Statehood</li>
    <li>Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 style="color: #064e3b;">Publication Frequency</h3>
  <p>JPD is published twice a year (Bi-annually) in January and July.</p>
