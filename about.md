  <section style="margin-bottom: 40px;">
    <h2 style="color: #064e3b; border-bottom: 2px solid #fbbf24; padding-bottom: 10px;">Our Vision</h2>
    <p>To become a leading academic hub in the Horn of Africa for research-driven solutions that promote sustainable peace, transparent governance, and inclusive socio-economic development.</p>
  </section>

  <section style="margin-bottom: 40px;">
    <h2 style="color: #064e3b; border-bottom: 2px solid #fbbf24; padding-bottom: 10px;">Our Mission</h2>
    <p>The Journal of Peace and Development (JPD) seeks to provide a platform for scholars, practitioners, and policymakers to share evidence-based research that fosters institutional accountability and social stability.</p>
  </section>

  <section style="margin-bottom: 40px; background: #f9f9f9; padding: 25px; border-radius: 12px; border: 1px solid #eee;">
    <h2 style="color: #064e3b; margin-top: 0;">Aims & Scope</h2>
    <p>We welcome original research articles and policy briefs in the following areas:</p>
    <ul style="padding-left: 20px;">
      <li><strong>Good Governance:</strong> Institutional building, rule of law, and anti-corruption.</li>
      <li><strong>Peace-building:</strong> Conflict resolution, community reconciliation, and security sector reform.</li>
      <li><strong>Sustainable Development:</strong> Economic policies, resource management, and social progress.</li>
      <li><strong>Traditional Leadership:</strong> The role of elders and customary law in modern state-building.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section style="text-align: center; padding: 30px; background: #eef9f1; border-radius: 15px;">
    <h3 style="color: #159957; margin-top: 0;">Open Access Policy</h3>
    <p style="font-size: 0.95em;">JPD is an <strong>Open Access</strong> journal, meaning all content is freely available without charge to the user or their institution. We believe in the free exchange of knowledge to support global progress.</p>
  </section>
