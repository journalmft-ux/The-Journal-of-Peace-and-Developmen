  <h1 style="color: #064e3b; text-align: center; border-bottom: 3px solid #fbbf24; padding-bottom: 10px;">Aims and Scope</h1>

  <p style="font-size: 1.1em; text-align: justify; margin-top: 30px;">
    The <strong>Journal of Peace and Development (JPD)</strong> is a peer-reviewed academic publication dedicated to exploring the complex intersection of stability, governance, and socio-economic progress in the Horn of Africa. 
  </p>

  <div style="background: #f8fafc; border-left: 5px solid #064e3b; padding: 20px; margin: 30px 0;">
    <h3 style="color: #064e3b; margin-top: 0;">Main Research Pillars:</h3>
    <ul style="list-style-type: square;">
      <li><strong>Peace & Conflict Transformation:</strong> Analyzing traditional mediation (Xeer), modern peace-building strategies, and community resilience.</li>
      <li><strong>Governance & State-building:</strong> Exploring the linkage between traditional leadership (Odayaal) and formal government institutions.</li>
      <li><strong>Sustainable Development:</strong> Researching economic growth, policy frameworks, and social infrastructure in developing regions.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <h3 style="color: #064e3b;">Our Vision</h3>
  <p>
    JPD aims to bridge the gap between academic research and policy-making. We provide a platform for Somali and international scholars to document evidence-based findings that contribute to the stability and prosperity of Somaliland and the wider region.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #064e3b;">Editorial Policy</h3>
  <p>
    We uphold the highest standards of academic integrity. Every article published in JPD undergoes a rigorous double-blind peer review process to ensure quality, originality, and relevance.
  </p>

</div>
