  <p style="margin: 5px 0; color: #555;">The Journal of Peace and Development (JPD)</p>

<div style="margin: 30px auto; max-width: 800px; font-family: 'Georgia', serif; line-height: 1.8; color: #333;">

  <h2 style="color: #064e3b; border-bottom: 2px solid #eee;">Mission and Vision</h2>
  <p>
    The <strong>Journal of Peace and Development (JPD)</strong> is an independent, peer-reviewed academic journal established in 2026. Our mission is to provide a high-quality platform for scholars, researchers, and practitioners to publish original research that contributes to the understanding of peacebuilding, governance, and sustainable development, with a specific focus on Somalia and the Horn of Africa.
  </p>

  <h2 style="color: #064e3b; border-bottom: 2px solid #eee; margin-top: 30px;">Aims and Scope</h2>
  <p>JPD welcomes interdisciplinary research in the following areas:</p>
  <ul style="list-style-type: square; padding-left: 20px;">
    <li>Public Administration and Policy Analysis</li>
    <li>Conflict Resolution and Peace Studies</li>
    <li>Traditional Leadership and Modern Governance</li>
    <li>Sustainable Development and Environmental Policy</li>
    <li>Global Tribalism and Institutional Integrity</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #064e3b; border-bottom: 2px solid #eee; margin-top: 30px;">Open Access Policy</h2>
  <p>
    JPD is an <strong>Open Access</strong> journal, which means that all content is freely available without charge to the user or their institution. Users are allowed to read, download, copy, distribute, print, search, or link to the full texts of the articles in this journal for any lawful purpose.
  </p>

  <div style="background: #eef7f3; padding: 20px; border-left: 5px solid #064e3b; margin-top: 40px;">
    <h3 style="margin-top: 0; color: #064e3b;">Publication Frequency</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 0;">JPD is published <strong>bi-annually</strong> (Two issues per year). Special issues may be published to cover specific conferences or thematic areas.</p>
  </div>
