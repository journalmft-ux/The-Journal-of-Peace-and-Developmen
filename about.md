# About The Journal of Peace and Development (JPD)

The **Journal of Peace and Development (JPD)** is an independent, open-access academic platform. Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic research and practical policy-making in the fields of governance, stability, and socio-economic progress.

### 🎯 Our Mission
To foster a culture of research and evidence-based leadership in the Horn of Africa by providing a high-quality peer-review platform for scholars and practitioners.

### 🏛️ Our Values
* **Excellence:** We maintain high academic standards through rigorous peer review.
* **Integrity:** We promote ethical research and transparent governance.
* **Accessibility:** All our publications are Open Access, making knowledge free for everyone.

### 🌍 Scope of Research
We welcome manuscripts in the following areas:
1. **Public Administration** and Institutional Accountability.
2. **Conflict Resolution** and Local Governance.
3. **Economic Development** and Sustainable Growth.
