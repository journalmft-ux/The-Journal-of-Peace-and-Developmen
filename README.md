## 💡 About the Journal
The **Journal of Peace and Development** is a dedicated platform exploring the critical links between **Good Governance**, **Sustainable Peace**, and **Socio-Economic Progress**. We believe that lasting peace is only possible when built on the foundation of transparent and accountable leadership.

## 🎯 Our Focus Areas
* **Good Governance:** Transparency, rule of law, and institutional accountability.
* **Peace-building:** Conflict resolution and community reconciliation.
* **Sustainable Development:** Economic growth that supports long-term stability.
* **Ethical Leadership:** The role of leadership in rebuilding societies.
