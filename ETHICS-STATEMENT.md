# Publication Ethics and Malpractice Statement

The Journal of Peace and Development (JPD) is committed to maintaining the highest standards of publication ethics. 

### 1. Duties of Editors
* **Fair play:** Manuscripts are evaluated for their intellectual content without regard to race, gender, or political philosophy of the authors.
* **Confidentiality:** Editors must not disclose any information about a submitted manuscript to anyone other than the corresponding author and reviewers.

### 2. Duties of Reviewers
* **Contribution to Editorial Decisions:** Peer review assists the editor in making editorial decisions.
* **Promptness:** Any selected referee who feels unqualified to review the research should notify the editor.

### 3. Duties of Authors
* **Originality:** Authors must ensure that they have written entirely original works.
* **Plagiarism:** Plagiarism in all its forms constitutes unethical publishing behavior and is unacceptable.
* **Multiple Publication:** An author should not publish manuscripts describing essentially the same research in more than one journal.
