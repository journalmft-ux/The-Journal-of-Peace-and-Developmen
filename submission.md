### 📤 How to Submit
To ensure a successful peer-review process, please follow these steps:

1.  **Format:** Ensure your manuscript is in Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx) format.
2.  **Email:** Send your completed manuscript and the details above to: **[journalmft@gmail.com](mailto:journalmft@gmail.com)**.
3.  **Review:** Our editorial board will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and provide a feedback within 30 days.
