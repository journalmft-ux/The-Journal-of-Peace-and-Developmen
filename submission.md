### 📋 Submission Details
*Fadlan macluumaadkan soo raaci maqaalkaaga markaad soo dirayso:*

| Field | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Topic/Title** | Full title of your research paper |
| **Level of Study** | Undergraduate / Masters / PhD |
| **Institution** | Your University or Organization |
| **Author Name** | Full legal name of the corresponding author |
| **E-mail** | A valid email for communication |

### ⚖️ Publication Policies
* **Originality:** Maqaalku waa inuu ahaado mid cusub oo aan meel kale lagu daabacin.
* **Open Access:** Waxaan taageernaa faafinta aqoonta si bilaash ah.
* **Review:** Guddiga tafatirka waxay kugu soo jawaabayaan **48 saac** gudahood, natiijada kama dambaysta ahna waa **30 casho**.
